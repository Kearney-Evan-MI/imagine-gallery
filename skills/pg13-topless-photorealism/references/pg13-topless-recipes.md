# PG-13 Topless Recipes

Load only when writing a new generation prompt. Keep SKILL.md rules first.

## Default locks

- Age — late 20s to early 30s
- Phenotype — British Isles features, Southern English phenotype, Dorset dark-haired type unless the user asked Hampshire
- Face — refined bone structure, softer oval face, delicate jawline, narrower midface
- Expression — tired amusement, mid-sentence, glance toward an implicit person about five feet off-frame
- Body — pick slim or ordinary, never both
- Weather for pool or beach — part-cloudy late season
- Crop — five-foot talking distance, head through hips, environment still visible

## Slim versus ordinary (paste one)

Slim
lean adult frame, ordinary shoulders, modest natural breast volume matching a slim ribcage, flat-to-gentle midsection, no fashion-thin gap, no stacked chest

Ordinary
average adult frame, soft natural midsection, natural breast volume matching an ordinary torso, unposed waist, not slim-fashion and not plus-size

## Scene bank (pick one, do not stack)

### Pool-edge talk (generic, not Harford)
Seated on a pool edge, bikini bottoms, mug or towel in hand, look off-screen, late-season part-cloudy light, damp hairline, 35mm f/2.8 from five feet, head-through-hips.

### After-swim sit
Sitting on a towel at the pool or lake edge, bottoms still on, hair darker where wet, available overcast light.

### Beach walk pause
Standing on damp sand at the waterline, shorts or bikini bottoms, arms loose, wind moving individual hair strands, overcast end-of-summer light.

### Porch morning
Wooden porch step, mug, open shade, hair unstyled from sleep, bottoms or soft shorts, high-overcast daylight.

### Lake dock
Sitting on weathered wood, feet toward water, look across the lake.

## Camera pick one

- pool, dock, beach, yard — Canon EOS R5, 35mm f/2.8, about five feet, eye-level medium-wide, head through hips
- conversational medium — Canon EOS R5, 50mm f/2.8, about five feet, same crop
- do not use 85mm f/1.4 for topless frames

## Tightening checklist

- Adult age band present
- One phenotype lock present
- One body block only, slim or ordinary
- Pose-matched breast language, volume follows the torso
- Five-foot medium-wide crop stated
- f/2.8 to f/4 stated, not f/1.4
- Late-season weather stated on outdoor water scenes
- One ordinary action or prop present
- Pores + subsurface scattering present
- PG-13 negatives last
- No minor wording
