# Pg13 Topless Photorealism

## Overview

Prompt-craft layer for new Grok Imagine frames of adult women in casual social partial nudity. Goal is a first-person documentary still — what a person standing there would actually see — not a lingerie ad, not porn, not cartoon anatomy.

Load photoreal-phenotype-prompts and pretty-women-photorealism first. This skill adds the PG-13 register, five-foot crop lock, late-season weather, slim-versus-ordinary body blocks, and scene recipes.

Never use for anyone described as under 18.

Harford County backyard pool-party continuity stays on pg13-topless-restyle. Do not import that yard, stone coping mug-scene, or named party into a generic generation unless the user explicitly calls that special occasion.

## Core rule

The scene is doing something ordinary with the top off. The body is present the way gravity and posture put it. Do not write the frame as if the point of the photo is the breasts.

Ethological test — if a friend or an implicit photographer could stand about five feet away and the moment would still make social sense (pool talk, after-swim sit, beach pause, tired joke, end-of-season swim), keep it. If the pose only exists for a camera, rewrite it.

## Required prompt order

1. Subject + adult age band + phenotype lock
2. Face structure and expression (conversational, not seductive)
3. Hair as individual strands
4. One body block only — slim or ordinary, never both, never stacked-glamour
5. Body and breasts in posture language, not catalog language
6. Remaining clothing, exact pose, hands, prop
7. Environment plus late-season weather when the brief is pool or beach
8. Camera, distance, crop, and depth of field (heaviest weight — this is what stops headshot collapse)
9. Skin and material realism anchors
10. Short negative closer including the PG-13 closer and no-headshot closer

## Age, phenotype, pretty

- Default age band late 20s to early 30s. State it.
- Default phenotype for this skill's recent set — Dorset / Southern English, dark wavy hair, British Isles features.
- Encode pretty as bone structure, pores, light, and quiet symmetry. Do not stack beautiful stunning gorgeous supermodel.

## Slim versus ordinary body block (pick one)

Slim block — lean adult frame, ordinary shoulders, modest natural breast volume matching a slim ribcage, flat-to-gentle midsection, longish waist, no fashion-thin gap, no bodybuilder cut, no stacked glamour chest

Ordinary block — average adult frame, soft natural midsection, natural breast volume matching an ordinary torso, unposed belly and waist, not slim-fashion and not plus-size unless asked, no oil, no snatched waist

One block per frame. Do not write slim-and-stacked.

## Late-season weather lock (default for pool or beach)

part-cloudy late season daylight, high broken cloud with enough sun left to hold a tan and swim before the rainy winter, not golden-hour syrup, not storm-dark, not midsummer blaze

## Five-foot crop and anti-headshot lock (required)

Always state all four

- Camera distance about five feet / 1.5 meters
- Crop — eye-level medium or medium-wide, head through hips or mid-thigh, environment still in frame
- Depth of field thicker than portrait-bokeh — 35mm or 50mm at f/2.8 to f/4
- Expression naturalistic — tired, amused, mid-sentence, glance toward the implicit person off-frame

Ban 85mm f/1.4 and iPhone portrait mode unless the user asked for a clothed or face-only portrait.

## PG-13 register

Keep talking, sitting, standing, walking, holding a mug, towel, or phone. Ban nipple jewelry, oil, wet-look glaze, arched-back lens posing, hands on breasts, beauty-dish, implied sex act, and long erotic prose inside the prompt.

## Photoreal anchors (required)

- photorealistic photograph / real unedited photo / candid photograph
- shot on Canon EOS R5 with 35mm f/2.8 or 50mm f/2.8 from about five feet, medium or medium-wide crop
- natural skin texture with visible pores and subtle imperfections
- realistic subsurface scattering
- unedited documentary style
- sharp focus on the eyes

## Minimal negatives (end only)

no cartoon, no anime, no illustration, no 3D render, no CGI, no plastic skin, no airbrushed skin, no beauty filter, no porcelain skin, no over-smoothed skin, no Central European features, no broad face, no pornographic posing, no glamour shoot, no looking at camera seductively, no oiled skin, no hands on breasts, no tight headshot, no face-only crop, no 85mm bokeh portrait, no underage, no child, no text, no watermark

## Workflow

1. Confirm adult. Refuse any minor wording.
2. Identify phenotype, age band, setting, and whether this is a new generation or an edit of a prior frame.
3. If a prior image ID exists and the user wants the same woman, stop and use pg13-topless-restyle instead.
4. Pick one scene recipe from references/pg13-topless-recipes.md or adapt the user setting.
5. Rebuild in the required order. Keep the prompt photographic and short.
6. Offer one tightened variant.
7. Prefer render_generated_image for a new frame. Prefer render_edited_image when identity must lock to a prior output.

## Anti-patterns

- Do not invent a second on-screen person unless asked
