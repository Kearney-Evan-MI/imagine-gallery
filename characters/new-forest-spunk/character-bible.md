# Character Bible: New Forest Spunk

**Character:** New Forest Spunk  
**Current Version:** v1.0.0 (2026-08-16)  
**Status:** Active

---

**Name / Alias:** (unnamed – photorealistic original character)  
**Regional Lock:** Hampshire / Solent / New Forest type (scrappier coastal Southern English, not polished Winchester graduate)

**Core Look**
- Hair: Messy, wind-blown curly-to-wavy medium brown hair, shoulder length or slightly shorter, natural and untamed
- Eyes: Light eyes with a slightly amused, knowing expression
- Skin: Fair-to-light with visible freckles across face, shoulders and chest; natural summer colour
- Smile / Expression: Soft smirk or half-smile, playful, slightly irreverent, not haughty
- Build: Lean-athletic / active outdoor type, natural proportions, visible collarbones and realistic soft definition

**Signature Clothing & State**
- Default summer heatwave look: topless or very lightly covered, light-coloured shirt or sweater tied around the waist, mid-rise or classic blue jeans
- Simple pendant necklace
- Overall impression of someone who has simply decided the heat is not worth the fuss of a top

**Personality / Mood**
- Scrappy, free-spirited, quietly amused
- Calm competence in the heat
- Cute rather than glamorous; the kind of face that becomes funnier the longer you look at the smirk and the wind-blown hair
- Not performative or self-conscious

**Recurring Settings & Theme**
- Open grassy fields, light woodland edges, seasonal outdoor gatherings
- “Weird European seasonal festival in the wilder areas” energy (New Forest, Solent fringe, or similar coastal Hampshire / southern English wild spaces)
- Strong natural daylight, summer heatwave atmosphere
- Broader framing: English women handling the heatwaves of 2026 by simply carrying on calmly

**Pose & Body Language**
- Relaxed standing or slight walk toward camera
- Arms natural at sides or loosely swinging
- Direct but easy eye contact
- Unforced, slightly wind-affected posture

**Technical Notes for Grok Imagine**
- Strong identity lock on the messy curly hair, freckle pattern, and soft smirk
- Prefer natural outdoor daylight and realistic skin texture with visible pores and subtle summer sheen
- Keep the Hampshire / Solent scrappy quality — avoid overly refined or “Winchester polished” features
- Style: photorealistic, candid, documentary summer festival feel
